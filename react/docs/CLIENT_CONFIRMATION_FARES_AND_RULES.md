## 4. Outstation Round-Trip Fare Rules

For custom multi-day and round-trip journeys outside Agra:

1. **Standard Tourism 300 KM / Day Benchmark:**
   - Multi-day outstation bookings are billed at a minimum average of **300 KM per calendar day** (or actual distance driven, whichever is greater).
   - *Example:* A 3-day round trip in an Ertiga (₹14/km) has a minimum billing base of `3 days × 300 km = 900 km × ₹14 = ₹12,600`.
2. **Same-Day Round Trip Multiplier (1.85×):**
   - For same-day return trips on standard routes, the round-trip fare is calculated as `Base One-Way × 1.85`.
   - *Example:* Agra–Delhi one-way Sedan (₹3,499) → Same-day round trip = `₹6,473` (approx ₹6,500).
3. **Tolls, Parking & State Tax on Round-Trips:**
   - On round-trip bookings, highway toll taxes, parking slips, and inter-state passenger commercial tax (e.g. Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Haryana) are paid directly by the passenger at actuals.

## 5. Driver Night Allowance Policy

A flat driver night allowance is charged when driving or picking up passengers late at night.

* **Night Window:** Pickups or operational duty between **08:00 PM (20:00) and 06:00 AM (06:00)**.
* **Charges:**
  * **Sedan / Ertiga / Innova Crysta:** **₹300 flat per night**
  * **Tempo Traveller / Force Urbania:** **₹500 flat per night**
* **Exemptions:** Pre-booked local sightseeing packages (8hr/12hr) and all-inclusive signature tour packages do not incur extra night charges unless the trip extends beyond the contracted package timeline.

## 6. Advance Booking Deposit & Balance Payment Rules

To guarantee vehicle allocation and avoid bogus reservations without burdening customers:

1. **Advance Booking Deposit:**
   - **Formula:** **28% of total fare**, rounded to the nearest ₹100.
   - **Minimum Advance:** **₹500** (ensures driver mobilization fee is covered).
   - **Maximum Advance:** Never exceeds 100% of the total fare.
   - *Examples:*
     - ₹1,900 Local Tour → 28% = ₹532 → Rounded = **₹500 advance** (₹1,400 balance)
     - ₹3,499 Delhi Drop → 28% = ₹980 → Rounded = **₹1,000 advance** (₹2,499 balance)
     - ₹18,500 Golden Triangle → 28% = ₹5,180 → Rounded = **₹5,200 advance** (₹13,300 balance)
2. **Remaining Balance Collection:**
   - The remaining balance is paid directly to the driver via cash or UPI at the start or completion of the trip.
3. **Customer Transparency:**
   - The advance amount and remaining balance are shown on the screen before the customer pays or books.

## 9. Cancellation & Customer Refund Policies

Clear rules protect your company from empty runs while giving genuine customers peace of mind.

### 9.1 Cab Bookings (One-Way, Round-Trip & Local Sightseeing)
* **Notice ≥ 24 Hours Before Scheduled Pickup:** **100% Full Refund** of the advance deposit (processed back to original bank/UPI account within 5–7 business days).
* **Notice < 24 Hours Before Pickup:** Advance deposit is retained to cover driver allocation, preparation, and vehicle reservation holding fees.
* **Customer No-Show at Pickup Location:** 0% refund.

### 9.2 Tour Packages Cancellation Schedule

| Notice Period Before Tour Start | Cancellation Fee (% Retained) | Customer Refund (% Returned) |
